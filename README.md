# The Notebook

**The Notebook** is a *romantic drama film* released in **2004**, directed by **Nick Cassavetes**.  
It’s based on the novel by **Nicholas Sparks** and stars **Ryan Gosling** and **Rachel McAdams** as young lovers separated by fate and time.

---

## Story Overview
The film follows **Noah Calhoun** and **Allie Hamilton**, two people from different social backgrounds who fall deeply in love during one unforgettable summer.  
Years later, they are reunited — proving that **true love never fades**, even after years apart.

---

### 🎬 Main Characters
1. **Noah Calhoun** – A passionate and hardworking man who restores an old house for Allie.  
2. **Allie Hamilton** – A wealthy young woman torn between love and family expectations.  
3. **Lon Hammond** – Allie’s fiancé, who loves her but can’t compete with her past love.

---

## Themes
- Everlasting love  
- Memories and aging  
- Choices and destiny
